Research Focus

Electrocatalysis & Water Splitting

Investigating phase evolution and reaction mechanisms of transition metal-based catalysts, specifically NiPx and anodized stainless steel mesh electrodes. Optimization is targeted for demanding environments, including alkaline 1M KOH and seawater media.

In-situ & Operando Spectroscopy

Employing in-situ Raman spectroscopy to analyze catalyst surface evolution (e.g., NiOOH phase shifts) and interfacial water dynamics. Utilizing NV center nanodiamonds via continuous-wave ODMR for quantum sensing of local electromagnetic fields.

Laboratory Automation & Workflows

Designing high-throughput synthesis and characterization pipelines. This includes integrating Python, Hitbot robotic arms, and custom ESP32/stepper motor hardware for liquid handling, 36-sample electrodeposition arrays, and autonomous screening.

Computational Modeling

Applying Density Functional Theory (DFT) using Quantum ESPRESSO and COMSOL Multiphysics to calculate Gibbs free energy profiles, model local fields, and correlate experimental spectroscopic data with interfacial double-layer phenomena.

Laboratory Automation in Action

Autonomous catalyst synthesis and screening setup developed alongside my collaborator, Utkarsh.
